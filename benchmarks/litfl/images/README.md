https://litfl.com/ecg-library/), kept here so the benchmark runs without depending on the site.
They are NOT covered by the repository's Apache-2.0 licence: each image remains the property of
its authors and of LITFL, under LITFL's terms. They are used only to evaluate the digitizer
(research, non-commercial). Source of each file:
